{% trans "1. Your account is yours" %}

{% trans "You answer for the descriptions you enter. Check them, correct them when you find a mistake, and say so when something is uncertain." %}

{% trans "Upload or link an image or a file only if you hold the rights to it or have permission from the holder. Give the licence and the credit on the resource." %}
