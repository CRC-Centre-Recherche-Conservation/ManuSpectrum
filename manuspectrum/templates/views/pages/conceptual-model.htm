{% blocktrans trimmed %} Most heritage databases record what an object is. ManuSpectrum records what happened to it — and who said so. Every measurement is an event with a date, an instrument and an operator. Every interpretation is stored separately from the observation that supports it. That separation is what lets you re-read someone else's conclusion — and disagree with it. {% endblocktrans %}
