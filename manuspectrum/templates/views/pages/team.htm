{% trans "The project & the people" %}

{% trans "ManuSpectrum is developed within Cluster 2 — “Consideration and searchability of materials-analysis data” — of the ANR Biblissima+ programme, at the Centre de Recherche sur la Conservation (CRC), in partnership with Huma-Num. It archives, standardises and makes searchable the physicochemical analysis of manuscripts and written heritage, so that non-specialists can reuse reliable, interoperable data." %}

{% trans "Read more" %}

{% trans "Built on Arches, ManuSpectrum places the material component at the pivot between the object, its context, the analyses and their interpretations, using shared ontologies (CIDOC-CRM, CRMsci) and reference thesauri to keep heterogeneous documents comparable and interoperable with the Biblissima portal." %}
