# Code of conduct

ManuSpectrum adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: we want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their background,
discipline or level of experience. Be kind, be patient, assume good faith, and give and receive feedback on the work,
never on the person.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and every other space of the project, and when someone represents
the project in public.

## Reporting

If you witness or experience behaviour that breaks this code, write to the maintainers privately through the
[contact page](https://manuspectrum.huma-num.fr/en/about/contact) of the platform. Reports are read by the project
team only and handled confidentially. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or
permanently exclude anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate, as the Contributor Covenant describes.
