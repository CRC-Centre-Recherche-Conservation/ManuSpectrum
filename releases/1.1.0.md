# ManuSpectrum 1.1.0 release notes

ManuSpectrum 1.1.0 is a feature release of the Data Lab.

## Changes

- Data Lab: the title of a Selection item opens it in its document, on its page, with its zone drawn and its card
  (analysis or identified material) open (#339). A Ctrl, Cmd, Shift or middle click opens it in a new tab instead. The
  browser's Back returns to where the click came from, and closing the card leaves the visitor on the same page. A row
  says when its analysis is not located on a page, and a material row shows its document. « Open in the document »
  in Compare's focus legend and « Open the analysis » in the « Without visualisation » window behave the same way; the latter
  no longer opens a new tab. A document opened from Compare offers « Back to Compare ».

## Upgrading

Nothing beyond the usual update: no migration, no data step.
