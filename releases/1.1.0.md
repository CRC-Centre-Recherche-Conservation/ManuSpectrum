# ManuSpectrum 1.1.0 release notes

ManuSpectrum 1.1.0 is a feature release of the Data Lab.

## Changes

- IIIF: every Document and Component has its own manifest, `/iiif/v3/manifest/<uuid>` (Presentation 3) and
  `/iiif/v2/manifest/<uuid>` (Presentation 2.1), built from the same facts as its annotation collection: the pages
  that carry an analysis or an identified material, each referencing its annotation pages, and a header that says
  what the document is (the fields of its summary), what was analysed (techniques, projects, dates, chemical imaging),
  who publishes it and where to download the data. It is filtered for its reader like the annotation pages, and
  `/iiif/v3/explorer-manifest?document=<uuid>` now answers a permanent redirect to it.
- IIIF: the Explorer manifests of a selection or a project carry the same header, and their label counts the analyses
  and pages (« Selection of 12 analyses on 1 page of … »). Imaging manifests are referenced by their address in the
  header instead of having their layer canvases copied into the manifest.

## Upgrading

Nothing beyond the usual update: no migration, no data step.
