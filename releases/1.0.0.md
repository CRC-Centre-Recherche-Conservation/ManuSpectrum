# ManuSpectrum 1.0.0 release notes

ManuSpectrum 1.0.0 is the first public release: the platform opens at
[manuspectrum.huma-num.fr](https://manuspectrum.huma-num.fr/) with the physicochemical analyses of written heritage
recorded at the Centre de Recherche sur la Conservation (CRC) and its partners.

It runs on **Arches 8.1.5** (Django 5.2, Python 3.11 to 3.13) and is released as a signed container image.

## Major features

### A data model for heritage science

- Twelve resource models built on **CIDOC-CRM** and **CRMsci**: Project, Document, Component, Analysis,
  Characterization, Sample, Alteration, Modification, Instrument, Person, Group and Place.
- An analysis is tied to the document, the folio (IIIF canvas) and the exact zone it was measured on; an identified
  material (Characterization) cites the analyses it rests on.
- Controlled lists in **SKOS** with English and French labels, shipped in a separate public
  [data package](https://github.com/CRC-Centre-Recherche-Conservation/manuspectrum-pkg) (CC BY, no business data),
  loaded and checked against its inventory at installation.
- Every resource is named and described by one project function (Multi Resource Descriptor), and the main models carry a
  configurable summary card.

### Data entry

- **New Project** workflow, and **Import Biblissima**: documents found in the Biblissima portal are created with their
  identifiers, holding institution, IIIF manifests and illuminations, polite to the upstream service (rate limit,
  cache, budgets per request).
- **XY spectra**: a file renderer that reads CSV exports with stored configurations per technique (columns, axes,
  reference normalisation, dark subtraction, treatments); configurations are stamped on upload by database triggers,
  whatever the write path.
- **Imaging layers**: each canvas of a chemical-imaging manifest can be labelled by element, emission line, band and
  processing; labels are proposed from the canvas names.
- File cards that mix formats (spectra, PDF, images) mount the right viewer for each file.

### The Analysis Explorer

A public Vue 3 application at `/{lang}/discover`:

- **Corpus**: search and facets (technique, material, colour, component, place with its hierarchy, period), with a
  per-document view that keeps or hides what the filters exclude.
- **Document**: the folio with its analysed zones, analysis and component cards, and their links.
- **Selection** kept in the browser, shared across tabs.
- **Compare**: one window per kind of measurement, laid out on a grid; every readable spectrum of the Selection, full
  resolution, with the treatments of the XY renderer; a linked focus (up to four pins) that highlights the same
  analysis, material or zone in every window; coverage matrix, periodic table, materials table and folio tool.
- **Light table** for imaging: a gallery of every canvas, one, two or four synchronised panes, a curtain between any two
  canvases and false-colour stacks of elemental maps, each image at its served scale.
- **Map and timeline** of the documents' production places and dates.
- **Share and export**: localised citations, a IIIF manifest of the scope, all spectra as one CSV (never decimated) and
  a downloadable **RO-Crate 1.2** package with its files and metadata.

### IIIF

- Annotation collections and pages per document and component, single annotations, characterizations, and **Content
  State** links to one zone, in **Presentation 3 / Web Annotation** and **Presentation 2.1 / Open Annotation**.
- Every language in one response; spectra as clean CSV datasets described by **`xyReading`**, a small documented IIIF
  extension (JSON-LD context, JSON Schema) that tells a viewer how to plot them.
- **IIIF Authorization** (1.0 and 2.0 probe) for restricted files, with short-lived read-only tokens.
- Read by [mirador-xyviewer](https://github.com/CRC-Centre-Recherche-Conservation/mirador-xyviewer), the companion
  Mirador 4 plugin, and by any IIIF viewer.

### Public pages

A bilingual homepage, About pages (project, team, conceptual model, interactive model graph, data and reuse), legal
notice, privacy policy and terms of use, without trackers. Basemaps come from OpenFreeMap, without a
Mapbox account.

### Accounts and data protection

- No self-signup: accounts are created by an administrator and activated by an invitation link; terms of use accepted
  at first sign-in, again when they change.
- Two-factor sign-in (TOTP), enforceable; lockout after repeated failures; password reset and two-factor reset by mail.
- Roles by group: editors, reviewers, exporters, graph editors, site managers.
- **Embargoes** of at most twelve months, with notices and automatic lifting; per-field restrictions respected by every
  public product.
- Daily passes: abandoned workflows cleaned after 90 days, inactive accounts anonymised after 24 months (with notice),
  login records kept six months.

### Operations

- One container image, signed with **cosign** (keyless) and scanned by **Trivy** before release; release candidates
  (`-rc.N`) from `dev`, final tags from `main`.
- **Docker Compose** stack: web (gunicorn, threaded), Celery worker and beat, PostgreSQL / PostGIS, Elasticsearch,
  Redis, Cantaloupe, nginx with TLS (Let's Encrypt), rate limits and edge rules.
- Host installed by **Ansible** (Ubuntu 24.04 and 26.04): firewall, unattended upgrades, time sync, mail relay, timers,
  idempotent re-runs.
- **Backups** with restic: nightly, 7 daily / 4 weekly / 6 monthly, a weekly restore test, a blocking backup before
  each update.
- **Monitoring**: Prometheus, Alertmanager and Grafana (dashboards, alert runbooks, mail by category), health and
  readiness probes, JSON logs with request ids and redaction, a monthly activity report.
- **Updates**: the host watches for new signed releases, mails the administrators, and installs only on their command
  (now or at a planned time, with a site notice), with an automatic rollback when the new version does not come up.

## Known issues

- In the resource editor, « Search for resources » can log `t.nodeid is not a function` in the browser console
  (Arches core); the search still works.

## Installing

This is the first release: there is nothing to upgrade from. Follow [`deploy/README.md`](../deploy/README.md) for a new
host (Ansible, then `make init`), and [`deploy/RELEASES.md`](../deploy/RELEASES.md) to verify the image signature by hand.
